AI & automation

At home.pl I built a QA agent that writes scenarios and Playwright tests, compares master with the branch, comments on merge requests and builds an HTML report, plus an automated delivery process with a human in the loop that turns labelled Jira tasks into reviewed merge requests. I write the skills, instructions and agents behind them myself, including a “resolve ticket” skill that reads Figma and builds components and sections in Tailwind.
