Multi-brand sales sites built from one page creator

The sales sites of a hosting group with 8 brands: more than 700 landing pages that users compose from predefined sections in a headless CMS, built by Gatsby.

CMS

Problem

The home.pl frontend team builds the marketing and sales sites of a multi-brand hosting group: home.pl, az.pl, strefa.pl and IONOS in Romania, Bulgaria, Czechia, Slovakia and Hungary. Every brand needs landing pages, offers and sales paths, and the pages are put together by users, not developers.

What we built

  • Content in a headless CMS, pages built by Gatsby. Almost everything on the sites is stored in the CMS and generated by Gatsby through GraphQL.
  • A generic page creator. Users compose a page in the CMS from a set of predefined sections, and Gatsby builds it for every brand.
  • More than 700 landing pages across 8 brands, plus the sales paths behind them: upsells, baskets and product configurators.
  • A new CMS and more brands. In 2019 there was one IONOS brand and the content lived in Contentful. Since then it has moved to Squidex, and the sites now cover 8 brands.
  • A frontend library in transition. The library on styled-components was already there in 2019, together with Gatsby; we used it and extended it for years. Now we are moving to Tailwind and our own library built from Figma tokens, with Storybook and multi-brand theming, the same approach as in my design system.

Highlights

  • Users, not developers, put the pages together: they pick predefined sections in the CMS, and Gatsby builds the page for every brand.
  • The sales paths are part of the same system: upsells, baskets and product configurators on the way from a landing page to an order.
  • I onboarded new developers into the project.
  • This system is the ground my AI work stands on: the QA agent and the human-in-the-loop delivery. The delivery runs on my “resolve ticket” skill, which reads Figma and builds components and sections in Tailwind step by step.
